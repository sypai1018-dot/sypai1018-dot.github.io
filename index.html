Research Vision

My long-term research aims to understand how representations evolve during learning and establish a general representation refinement framework that improves robustness, generalization, and efficiency.

Instead of proposing isolated algorithms, I seek transferable principles that can bridge different AI paradigms, including Medical AI, Efficient AI, General Vision, and future Trustworthy AI.
