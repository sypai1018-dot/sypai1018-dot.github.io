Research Overview

My research focuses on understanding representation dynamics in deep learning and developing principled refinement mechanisms that improve robustness, efficiency, and generalization.

My recent work spans representation learning, graph neural networks, medical AI, and efficient deep learning, aiming to establish a unified representation refinement framework across different learning paradigms.

Research Themes

Representation Dynamics

Understanding how feature representations evolve during learning and how refinement mechanisms improve robustness and generalization.

Graph Representation Learning

Graph neural networks, graph geometry, graph signal propagation, and medical graph learning.

Efficient Deep Learning

Lightweight architectures, model refinement, efficient inference, and practical AI deployment.

Trustworthy AI

Building robust, interpretable, and reliable AI systems through principled representation learning.

Research Programs

My current research is organized into four complementary research programs that collectively investigate the evolution, refinement, and application of learned representations.

CMMR

Compact Manifold Modulation and Refinement

A representation refinement framework designed to preserve local geometric structures while improving feature quality.

  • Representation Refinement
  • Feature Geometry
  • Deep Representation Learning

PRL

Medical Graph Representation Learning

Investigating representation dynamics in graph neural networks for medical image analysis and graph-based learning.

  • Graph Neural Networks
  • Medical AI
  • Representation Dynamics

TCS-II

Efficient Deep Learning

Developing lightweight representation refinement methods for efficient inference on resource-constrained computing platforms.

  • Efficient AI
  • Model Compression
  • Lightweight Networks
