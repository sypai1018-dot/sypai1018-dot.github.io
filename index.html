Research Questions

How do representations evolve during learning?

Understanding feature evolution in modern deep learning systems.

How can representations be refined without destroying semantic structures?

Developing general representation refinement mechanisms.

How can representation refinement improve practical AI systems?

Applications in Medical AI, Efficient AI, and General Vision.

How can representation refinement contribute to trustworthy AI?

Future directions toward robust, interpretable, and privacy-aware AI.

Research Vision

My long-term research aims to understand how representations evolve during learning and establish a general representation refinement framework that improves robustness, generalization, and efficiency.

Instead of proposing isolated algorithms, I seek transferable principles that can bridge different AI paradigms, including Medical AI, Efficient AI, General Vision, and future Trustworthy AI.
